# Bug hunt 2026-11-03

> **All fixed** 2026-11-04 on main (1c9a0299). Found at main 8c830a65 (2026-11-03). The hunt itself changed nothing in the code; the fixes are in <../Plans/Plan - Bug hunt 2026-11-03 fixes.md>. Not rolled out.
> In short: a second, deeper pass over the app's oldest code, chosen strategically: not every old line, but the old code the teacher leans on every day, where a mistake costs notes, marks or a child's place in a sequence. Four reviewers took one area each (the presentations core and the Checklist, the student page's progress and year plan, Work and practice, Todos and Today) and found 60 bugs; the worst are a one-click Delete in the presentation history that wipes notes and mastery marks, Checklist Unmark Presented and Remove Plan deleting a one-child presentation with its notes, the presentation sheet having no way to mark the lesson just given as mastered for one child, Unlock Next Lesson unlocking a child's whole group, and Save on an open work page undoing a Mastered set elsewhere.

"Confirmed" means the code path was traced end to end. "Plausible" means the path is real but the trigger depends on timing, data, or something Apple doesn't document. Paths are under `Cosmic Daybook/`.

## Scope: why these files

Every line of the app has now been hunted at least once by date: the pre-June lines on 10-10 and 10-11, June–September lines on 10-11, 10-12 and 10-17, everything since 09-30 on 10-29, and the data layer by topic on 11-02. "The oldest" therefore no longer means "unread". It means the code that has been read least recently, and only once, by a reviewer who had 12,000 lines to get through: the pre-June lines, 84,766 of the app's 303,941 at 8c830a65 (measured line by line with `git blame -w -M`, the only measure that survives the September rename and reorganizations).

Rather than spread four reviewers over all 84,766, the hunt picked the old code by what it costs when it's wrong:

- **Daily use and real writes.** Work and practice sessions (8,185 old lines in 55 files), the student page's progress, sequence recap, year plan, history and insights with the shared progression services and the Children view (8,261 in 46), Todos and Today (6,926 in 44), and the presentations core, lesson page and the Checklist's cell and batch actions (5,233 in 31). Together 28,600 lines, 176 files, each still 60–100 % its original lines.
- **Left out on purpose.** Notes and Quick Capture (8,884 old lines) had the hardest look of the 10-10 hunt (14 findings in Notes alone) and the fewest daily writes since; Stories, Book Club, Going Out, Procedures, Parsha, Topics, Resources and the AI planning drafts (about 12,000 old lines) are used rarely and write little; Backup's old transformers were re-read by topic on 11-02; the Daybook Assistant has no old code.
- **Each reviewer read the earlier reports for its area first**, so nothing here repeats a finding. Where a finding shows an earlier fix was only partly done, it says so and cites the number.

The hand checks before this was written: the history Delete (#1), Checklist Remove Plan (#2), the recap's current-lesson row (#18), Unlock on a group plan (#19), the work page's stale Save (#33) and Unlock twice (#34), Today's reload gate (#48) and Clear Completed (#50).

## What runs through all of it

- **Deletes that ask nothing and take notes with them.** The presentation history's Delete (#1), Checklist Unmark Presented and Remove Plan on a one-child presentation (#2), Remove in Merge Groups (#3), the work page's note, check-in and step trash buttons (#35), Clear Completed and batch Delete in Todos (#50, #51), Readjust on the year plan (#31). Every one is a single tap or click; the cell's own Clear, the Ready backlog's delete and Claude's `discard_presentation` all ask first and keep the notes, so the pattern to copy already exists.
- **Screens that read once and never look again.** The work page saves the status it loaded, not the one the row has now (#33), and offers Unlock against a list it never refreshes (#34); the Overview's Next Lessons (#25), Insights' Mark Reviewed (#30), Ready and Brewing after a lesson edit (#6), the Checklist's columns (#14), the Lesson Journey (#16), Today's reminders after a sync (#48), the Todos sidebar and the work cards after midnight (#57, #40).
- **The Checklist never learned what the presentation sheet does.** Marking a cell doesn't release her from the plan her year plan put her on (#4), doesn't count as confirmation for Presentations (#5), records a lesson with no presentation as given today (#8), and marks whichever history row comes first (#15); Add Sequence doesn't see Checklist masteries either (#23). Each is a rule the detail sheet got in October (10-11 #12, #17; 10-10 #64) that the Checklist's older paths skipped.
- **The year plan drifts from the presentations.** A promoted entry stays on its old day when the presentation moves (#20), a lesson given without promotion draws twice (#21), entries are numbered from wherever Add Sequence started (#24), and the Children view and timeline don't see year-plan entries at all (#28).
- **Per-child state on shared records.** Unlock Next Lesson flips `manuallyUnblocked` on a group plan (#19); "Working With" and Group Meeting read linked copies as one shared row (#36); the drill-in sheet and track timeline still read only work she owns (#26, finishing 10-29 #16).
- **Children who left, and hidden test students,** in practice pickers, Work History, to-do tag pickers, templates, Today's meetings and notes, Merge Groups, the Lesson Journey, Brewing's readiness, Lesson Cohort and Group Meeting bookings (#36, #38, #41, #55, #12, #16, #17, #32).

## Presentations, the lesson page and the Checklist

1. **Delete in the presentation history destroys a given presentation's notes and the children's mastery marks, without asking.** Confirmed (by hand), high. `Presentations/Assignments/LessonAssignmentHistoryView+Rows.swift:180-183`, `LessonAssignmentHistoryView+DataLoading.swift:104-110`, `Presentations/Workflow/PresentationRecordCleanup.swift:37-41`. The context menu's Delete runs at once: `prepareToDelete` removes every history row tied to the presentation, mastery marks included, and the notes cascade with the record (`unifiedNotes` is Cascade in the model). Unlike Remove Plan and moving a child between groups, it never calls `ChecklistRosterRemoval.keepMasteryMarks`, and the presentation's work is left pointing at nothing. The Ready backlog's delete and Claude's `discard_presentation` ask first and count the notes, but they handle plans only; this is the one path that deletes history. *Fix:* ask first, naming the notes and marks, and run `keepMasteryMarks` and the note-keeping `ChecklistCellRecords.clear` uses before the delete.
2. **Unmark Presented and Remove Plan in the Checklist delete the whole presentation and its notes when she was the only child on it.** Confirmed (by hand), medium-high. `Lessons/Checklist/Model/ChecklistRosterRemoval.swift:20-29`, `Lessons/Checklist/ViewModels/ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+CellActions.swift:117-137, 205-218`, `Lessons/Checklist/Views/ClassChecklistSmartCell.swift:155-167`, `ChecklistCellCard.swift:182`. A recorded one-child presentation, or a scheduled plan with notes on it, goes through `context.delete`; the notes cascade and nothing asks first. (The mastery marks are kept: `remove` calls `keepMasteryMarks` first.) The cell's own Clear asks, and keeps those notes as hers. *Fix:* send both through the note-keeping in `ChecklistCellRecords`, and ask first when the record is given or carries notes.
3. **Remove in Merge Groups deletes the plan and its notes when the last child is taken off.** Confirmed, medium. `Presentations/Overview/ConsolidatePresentationsSheet.swift:291-314`. `remove(studentID:)` is a hand-written copy of the roster removal: with nobody left it deletes the plan (notes cascade) with no question, and it skips `keepMasteryMarks`. Dragging the same chip onto another group goes through `PresentationGroupMove.apply`, which hands the notes to that group. *Fix:* call `ChecklistRosterRemoval.remove`, and confirm when the emptied plan has notes.
4. **Marking a lesson presented, mastered or practicing in the Checklist doesn't take her off the plan her year plan put her on.** Confirmed, medium. `ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+CellActions.swift:170-229`, `+LadderSteps.swift:14-41`, `Lessons/Checklist/Model/ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:182-243`. Maya is promoted into Thursday's group, gets the lesson today, and the teacher ticks Presented: she stays on Thursday's roster and is taught it again. None of these paths calls `YearPlanReleaseService.releaseRedundantPlans`, which `LifecycleService` and the detail view run. The same gap as 10-11 #12, fixed only in the detail view. *Fix:* run `releaseRedundantPlans` after each cell and batch mark.
5. **Presentations and the Checklist or Today judge readiness by two different rules.** Confirmed, medium. `Presentations/Planning/BlockingAlgorithmEngine.swift:241-245` vs `Planning/Services/ReadyForNextEngine+PreviousLesson.swift:112-114` (the split is noted at `ClassGrid/Engine/ClassGridEngine+Checklist.swift:181-185`). Presentations counts only a confirmation recorded on the presentation (`confirmedStudentIDs`); a Checklist Mastered never writes one, so the Checklist calls the next lesson Ready, its Plan makes a draft, and the draft sits in Presentations' Brewing as "needs confirmation". The other way round, Today and the Checklist ignore a lesson's "Requires Confirmation: No" and say "Not yet" where Presentations says Ready. This undoes the one-rule fix of 10-11 #17. *Fix:* one readiness rule, counting mastery as confirmation and honoring the lesson's settings.
6. **Ready and Brewing don't refresh after a lesson's rules, area or sequence, or position changes.** Confirmed, medium. `Presentations/Overview/PresentationsViewModel.swift:224-237`, `PresentationsView+Body.swift:13-16`. Lessons are hashed by id only (`computeIDHash(lessons)`), so a lesson edit leaves the hash unchanged and `updateAsync` returns early; sub-area defaults (`LessonSequenceSettings`) and work participants aren't watched at all. Turning off "Requires Practice", or reordering a sequence, leaves the backlog wrong until some presentation or work changes. *Fix:* hash the fields the engine reads (area, sequence, order, both rule overrides) and watch `LessonSequenceSettings` and `WorkParticipantEntity`.
7. **Moving a lesson to another sequence through the edit form keeps its old position, so it lands in the middle of the new sequence.** Confirmed, medium. `Lessons/Detail/LessonEditDraft.swift:84-86`; compare `Lessons/Library/LessonSequenceOrdering.swift:62-72` and `Lessons/AddLessonView.swift:243`. Dragging to another sub-area and Add Lesson both place the lesson last; the edit form keeps its old order number, so it ties with or jumps ahead of lessons already there, silently changing which lesson comes before which (and so readiness) and the first-lesson-is-key rule. No track is made for a new sub-area either. *Fix:* when area or sequence changes, place it with `nextOrderInSequence`, as Add Lesson does.
8. **Mastered, Practicing or Reviewing on a lesson with no presentation records it as given today.** Confirmed, medium. `ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+CellActions.swift:178-183, 235-266`, `+LadderSteps.swift:40`, `ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:219-224`. In September the teacher bulk-marks Mastered on lessons the children mastered last year; each gets a presentation dated today. The History log lists them as given today, and the Waiting rail and Suggest read every child as "taught today". Claude's `mark_mastered` refuses in the same situation. *Fix:* when she has no presentation, attach an undated one (`.undated`), as Previously Presented does.
9. **The marker showing which child is holding up a Brewing lesson often marks nobody.** Confirmed, low-medium. `Presentations/Planning/BlockingCacheBuilder.swift:101-109`, `Presentations/Overview/ReadyToPresentSection+Rows.swift:215`. The marker comes from a separate lookup that finds the earlier lesson's presentation only when it had exactly the same children; it takes the first match in fetch order and compares lesson ids as raw text. A group drawn from two earlier groups shows no marker though the readiness engine says it is blocked. *Fix:* take the blocking children from the engine's own per-child results.
10. **Add to Inbox on a selection can take children off plans.** Plausible, medium-low. `ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:22-31, 151-154`. The batch checks the grid as drawn, then runs the cell's toggle; if another device planned her since the grid was last drawn, the toggle finds her plan and removes her, even from a dated one. 10-17 #48 fixed this for batch Presented ("only ever adds") but not for this batch. *Fix:* the batch only adds, never toggles.
11. **Splitting the ready children off a Brewing plan leaves their notes and year-plan links on the old plan.** Confirmed, low-medium. `Presentations/Services/PresentationSplitService.swift:33-63` (from `ReadyToPresentSection+Actions.swift:36-48` and `Siri/MarkLessonPresentedIntent.swift:59-68`). Unlike `PresentationGroupMove`, it never calls `PresentationRecordCleanup.moveStudents`: notes about only Maya stay with the children still brewing, and her year-plan entry still points at a plan she is no longer on. *Fix:* call `moveStudents` for the ready children.
12. **A plan with a departed or hidden test child stays in Brewing for a reason nobody can see.** Plausible, low-medium. `BlockingAlgorithmEngine.swift:161-172`, `ReadyToPresentSection+Rows.swift:216-219, 245-247`. Readiness is judged over every id on the plan, but the chips hide those children, and "1 of 3 ready" counts them. Removing a departing child from her plans is only offered, not done, so this happens whenever the teacher declines. *Fix:* judge only enrolled, visible children.
13. **Checklist and backlog actions fail silently when a save fails.** Plausible, low. `ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+CellActions.swift:81`, `ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:31, 60, 87, 116, 134`, `ReadyToPresentSection+Actions.swift:24, 33, 47`. They use `context.safeSave()`, which only logs; the grid is redrawn from the unsaved changes, so it looks saved, and the changes wait for some unrelated save. *Fix:* save through `SaveCoordinator`.
14. **The Checklist's columns don't follow roster changes while it is open.** Confirmed, low. `ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel.swift:137-143, 197-201`. `refreshMatrix` never refetches students, though "Student" is in the list of synced changes that trigger a refresh; a child who leaves keeps her column, a new child doesn't appear until the screen is reopened. *Fix:* refetch the roster in `refreshMatrix`.
15. **Mastered in the Checklist marks whichever history row the store returns first.** Plausible, low. `ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+PresentationHelpers.swift:17-21`, `ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:282-284`. An unsorted single-row fetch; Claude's tools mark and read the latest row, so after a repeat presentation the latest row can read as not mastered. The 10-10 #64 bug, fixed in the detail view only. *Fix:* mark the latest row by date given.
16. **The Lesson Journey goes stale and counts hidden children.** Confirmed, low. `Lessons/Detail/LessonJourneyTimeline.swift:30-32, 91, 244-255`. It loads once per lesson, so a plan or presentation made from the card's Give Lesson doesn't appear; its counts and names include hidden test students and children who left. *Fix:* reload on presentation and work changes; filter the roster.
17. **Smaller.** Pages file Import and Remove in the edit form save at once, so Cancel doesn't undo them and the removed file is already in the Trash (`Lessons/Detail/LessonDetailView+FileHandling.swift:72-129`, `+EditForm.swift:110-116`). Add Lesson is fixed at 520 × 720, so on an iPhone the Add button can sit off screen (`AddLessonView.swift:61`, plausible). Merge Groups shows hidden test students' chips by name (`roster.byID` unfiltered, `ConsolidatePresentationsSheet.swift:182`). Presentation Info doesn't reload the per-child Re-present names after Edit (`LessonAssignmentDetailSheet.swift:401-405`).

## The student page: progress, recap, year plan, history and insights

18. **On the presentation sheet, the teacher can't mark the lesson she just gave as mastered for one child.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Students/Presentations/Recap/SequenceRecapBlocks.swift:57-60, 155-162`, `Students/Presentations/PresentationDetailViewModel.swift:304-309`. The 2026-10-01 redesign (6948166c) removed the group-wide Mastery row; its note says each child's mastery is now set from the sequence recap, and `isExpandable` says the current lesson's row is expandable "because it is where one child's mastery of this lesson is set". But `detailContent` shows the status picker only on the other lessons' rows (`if !entry.isCurrentLesson`), so the current lesson's row opens to nothing. `proficiencyState` has no control left on the sheet, so `updateProficiencyState` always gets `guideChangedState == false`. Mastery for the lesson being recorded can only be set from the Checklist or MCP. *Fix:* show the picker on the current row too; `updateSiblingLessonProficiencyState` already writes every row for that child and lesson.
19. **"Unlock next lesson" for one child unlocks her whole group.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Services/Progression/UnlockNextLessonService.swift:61-79`; caller `Work/Detail/WorkDetailView+Helpers.swift:276-293`; readers `Presentations/Planning/BlockingAlgorithmEngine.swift:122` and `ClassGrid/Engine/ClassGridEngine+Checklist.swift:125`. The service reuses any unpresented plan whose roster merely includes her (`isSubset`), a group draft or Thursday's scheduled group included, and sets the shared `manuallyUnblocked` flag. Presentations stops blocking the whole group, and the Checklist turns Etty's cell Ready though she hasn't finished practice on the lesson before. *Fix:* reuse a plan only when its roster is exactly her; otherwise split her onto her own unlocked draft.
20. **A year-plan entry stays on its original day after its presentation is moved.** Confirmed, medium. `Students/YearPlan/Services/YearPlanPromotionService.swift:17-44, 70-86`, `Planning/PresentationDropHandling.swift:133-144`, `Students/YearPlan/StudentYearPlanViewModel.swift:56-60, 112-124`. Promotion copies the day into `entry.plannedDate` once; a later drag looks only at entries still `.planned`, and nothing else writes `plannedDate` for a promoted entry. The year plan hides the linked presentation (`promotedIDs`) and draws the entry on its old date: struck "Promoted", then "Given" on a day nothing happened, and it can't be dragged. In `autoPromoteIfPlanExists`, a group plan is also scheduled to whichever child's entry date came last, while the others' entries keep their own dates. *Fix:* draw a promoted entry at its presentation's scheduled or given date, or update `plannedDate` whenever that presentation's day changes.
21. **A lesson given without its year-plan entry being promoted appears twice on the year plan.** Confirmed, medium-low. `StudentYearPlanViewModel.swift:51-60, 112-138`, `Students/YearPlan/YearPlanCalendarItem.swift:68-69`. Quick record, "Presented Today" on a Ready row, and MCP `record_presentation` don't promote the entry; it counts as given anyway (completion is derived), so the calendar draws the entry as "Given" on its planned day and the unlinked presented assignment as "Given" on the real day. *Fix:* when a given entry's lesson already has an unlinked presented assignment for her, draw it once, at the presentation's date.
22. **The sequence recap shows a mastered lesson as "Presented" after it is given again.** Confirmed, medium-low. `Students/Presentations/Recap/SequenceRecapDataLoader.swift:133-148`, `SequenceRecapResolver.swift:177-186`, `SequenceRecapSection.swift:133`, `Work/Services/LifecycleService.swift:66-75`. Each presentation makes a new `.presented` history row; the recap keeps only the newest row per child and lesson, so after a review regive the badge says Presented, "Mastered on" disappears, the "N / M mastered" chip drops, and the picker highlights Presented. Everywhere else any marked row counts (`PresentationRecordIndex.masteryMarks`). `firstPresentedAt` also holds the latest date. *Fix:* the highest state across her rows, `masteredAt` as the earliest mark, the first date as the earliest `presentedAt`.
23. **Add Sequence can start at, and plan, lessons she has already had.** Confirmed, medium-low. `Students/YearPlan/AddSequenceViewModel.swift:71-100, 124-171`. "First unpresented" reads only presented assignments, and the Checklist's Mastered writes only a history row (`ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+PresentationHelpers.swift:29-37`, `ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:293`). Lessons marked mastered on the Checklist become the starting point, get entries that read "Given" on future dates and push every real lesson back by one spacing step. *Fix:* use `YearPlanSatisfaction` for both the starting lesson and the preview, and skip satisfied lessons.
24. **Year-plan entries are numbered from wherever each Add Sequence started, so moving one moves the wrong lessons.** Confirmed, low-medium. `AddSequenceViewModel.swift:169, 204`, `StudentYearPlanViewModel.swift:164-171`, `YearPlanPacing.swift:86-93`. `orderInSequence` is the preview index (0 = the chosen start lesson); two runs, or a run on top of older auto-populated entries, give lessons 1 and 3 both order 0. Dragging lesson 1 then moves lessons 2 and 4 but not 3, and Readjust sorts out of order. *Fix:* store the lesson's own `orderInSequence`, or sort and cascade by it.
25. **The Overview's "Next Lessons" and "Working on" never refresh for changes made elsewhere.** Confirmed, low-medium. `Students/Detail/StudentDetailView.swift:298-311, 316-327`, `StudentDetailViewModel.swift:42-69`. The reloads run on `.onChange` of `vm.lessons` and `vm.lessonAssignments`, which change only when the view model itself reloads. On iPhone and iPad, closing a work row in the Overview's own work sheet leaves it under "Working on" (the sheet's `onDone` doesn't refetch); on the Mac, a lesson recorded in another window stays in "Next Lessons". The gap 10-10 #37 fixed on the Progress tab. *Fix:* `.onPresentationDataChangeWhenVisible(of: ["LessonAssignment", "WorkModel"])`, and refetch when the work sheet closes.
26. **The 10-29 #16 fix is incomplete: the drill-in sheet and the track timeline still read only work the child owns.** Confirmed, low-medium. `ClassGrid/Children/StudentSequenceDetailLoader.swift:91-104`, `Students/Progress/StudentSubjectProgressionViewModel.swift:191-195`. The Children card files shared project work by `WorkVisibility` (`ClassGridChildrenModel.swift:229-242`); the sheet it opens and the track timeline fetch `studentID == her` only. The card says Practicing while the sheet says "No work items" and the timeline says Presented. *Fix:* the same `WorkVisibility` / `WorkGrouping.visibleWork` rule in both.
27. **The drill-in sheet's Notes tab misses most lesson notes.** Confirmed, low-medium. `StudentSequenceDetailLoader.swift:149-164`. It reads only notes with `lessonID IN …`; notes made from the presentation and work editors link through `lessonAssignment`, `work` or `workCheckIn` and carry no `lessonID`. The recap had to fix this same gap (`SequenceRecapDataLoader.swift:97-114`). *Fix:* add those three paths to the predicate.
28. **The Children view and the student's timeline disagree with the Checklist.** Confirmed, low. `ClassGrid/Children/ClassGridChildrenModel.swift:108-131, 148-161`, `StudentSubjectProgressionViewModel.swift:79-81, 117-121`, against `Students/Progress/StudentChecklistState.swift:174-190`. Both ignore year-plan entries, so the lesson she's up to reads "Not Started" there and Planned on the Checklist; the timeline offers "Plan Lesson" on it, which silently schedules it onto the entry's date. A sequence where her only record is a Checklist mastery mark never appears on her Children card, since "active" counts only assignments and open work. *Fix:* include `isNextOnYearPlan`, and treat mastery marks as activity.
29. **The Progress card's "Next Lesson" is the first lesson not yet mastered, not the next one to give.** Confirmed, low. `Students/Progress/StudentProgressTabViewModel.swift:286-292`, `StudentProgressTab.swift:311-317`. A lesson she's practicing shows as "Next Lesson" while the timeline in the same sheet marks a later lesson Next. *Fix:* the timeline's rule (first lesson not given and not planned), or label it "Working toward".
30. **Insights' "Mark Reviewed" saves but the card doesn't change.** Confirmed, low. `Students/Insights/StudentInsightsView.swift:132-135`, `StudentInsightsView+Sections.swift:58-66, 133-137`. `isReviewed` is set on a Core Data object the view doesn't observe. *Fix:* reload `snapshots` after the save.
31. **Readjust rewrites every behind-pace sequence's dates with no confirmation and no undo.** Confirmed, low. `Students/YearPlan/StudentYearPlanTab.swift:37-50`, `StudentYearPlanViewModel.swift:200-230`. One tap re-lays whole strands from tomorrow, overwriting dates the teacher chose by hand. *Fix:* confirm with a count ("Move 14 lessons in 3 sequences?") or offer Undo.
32. **A child who has left can still get new plans.** Confirmed, low. `Students/Progress/StudentTrackDetailView.swift:296-303`, `StudentSubjectProgressionViewModel.swift:147-158`, `AddSequenceViewModel.swift:179-209`. "Plan Lesson" on a track timeline and Add Sequence on a withdrawn child's page create a draft or year-plan entries without the enrollment check `WorkRepository.createWork` has. *Fix:* hide both, or refuse with a plain message, when she isn't enrolled.

Smaller: a few error toasts use the first name alone ("Couldn't delete Maya…", "Couldn't load Maya's past insights") at `StudentDetailView.swift:284` and `StudentInsightsView.swift:90, 112`; failures in the recap's Add work and work-status buttons are only logged, with no message (`PresentationDetailViewModel+RecapWorkActions.swift:33-35`, `Work/Support/WorkRepository.swift:219-223`).

## Work and practice

33. **Save on an open work page can undo a status or title changed elsewhere.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Work/Detail/WorkDetailViewModel.swift:84-104`, `WorkDetailViewModel+Actions.swift:22-40`, `WorkDetailView.swift:120-127`. `loadWork` reads status and title once; the page's only refresh on a work change reloads the "Next Presentation" card. Maya's work is open in a Mac window; the teacher marks it Mastered from the grid (or Claude does through `update_work`, or another device syncs it); back in the window she types a note and presses Save. The page still holds "Working", so `save()` logs Working, which reopens the work: `completedAt` and participant completion cleared, the completion record left behind, the skipped check-ins still skipped. A stale title is written the same way, onto every linked copy. *Fix:* when the stored row changes, reload the header fields the teacher hasn't touched, or log a status only when she changed the pill.
34. **Unlock Next Lesson can make a second draft of the same lesson.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `WorkDetailView+Helpers.swift:262-297`, `Services/Progression/UnlockNextLessonService.swift:61-92`. When the next lesson has no assignment yet, Unlock creates a draft but never adds it to the page's `relatedLessonAssignments`; picking Mastered again, or tapping the Unlock pill, checks that stale list, offers the alert again, and the service (handed the same stale list) creates a second unblocked draft for the same child and lesson. Unlock saves at once, so Cancel on the page doesn't take it back. *Fix:* reload the page's assignments after unlocking, and look for an existing draft with a fresh fetch.
35. **Several delete buttons on the work page delete with one tap and no confirmation.** Confirmed, medium. `Work/Detail/NoteRowView.swift:43`, `WorkDetailView+Helpers.swift:299-305`, `WorkDetailView+Calendar.swift:168-171`, `Work/CheckIns/WorkCheckInRow.swift:62-66, 139-143`, `Work/Steps/WorkStepEditorSheet.swift:35-37`. The trash icon beside the pencil on each work note deletes the observation (it also shows on the child's Notes tab) and saves at once. A check-in's Delete removes the check-in and the note typed on it (that note's `work` is nil, so it goes with the check-in); `WorkCheckInService.unschedule` exists to keep such notes by skipping instead, and the page doesn't use it. Delete Step has no confirmation either. *Fix:* confirm each, and route the check-in delete through `unschedule`.
36. **"Working With", "Project Group" and Group Meeting treat linked copies as one shared row, and include children who left.** Confirmed, low-medium. `WorkDetailViewModel.swift:157-160, 183-190, 232-244`, `WorkDetailView+CompletionSection.swift:270-302, 406-409`, `WorkDetailView+Calendar.swift:176-252`. Quick New Work's copies name each other as participants, so Maya's copy lists Eli and Ana under "Working With"; their participant rows on her copy are never stamped (`WorkLogService.close` stamps only the children being logged), so they never show completed and appear again in Lesson Cohort. These lists use every student (`studentsByID`), not the enrolled roster, so departed children (and hidden test students in Cohort) appear and are put into Group Meeting bookings; the project fallback calls any classmate with "Project" work on the lesson part of the group. *Fix:* use `WorkGrouping` to show "Working With" only for genuinely shared rows, and filter all three lists to enrolled, visible children.
37. **A check-in added on a closed work's page shows up nowhere else.** Confirmed, low-medium. `WorkDetailView+Calendar.swift:149-166`. The Scheduled Check-Ins section is offered for every status, Keep Practicing included, which counts as closed; the strip and Today show check-ins only on open work for enrolled children (`CalendarForwardShiftService.onPlan`, `WeekPlanSection+Data.swift:241-245`). The check-in sits on the work page only and never comes due. *Fix:* hide the planner on closed work, or say the work has to be reopened first.
38. **Hidden test students appear in the practice pickers and in Work History.** Confirmed, low. `Work/Practice/RecordPracticeSheet.swift:16`, `Work/Practice/PracticeSessionSheet.swift:15, 64-76`, `Work/Agenda/WorksLogView.swift:77-108`. Both practice sheets read `roster.enrolled`, which doesn't apply the test-student filter; the Works log filters only its picker, not its rows. *Fix:* pass both rosters and the log's rows through `testStudents.visible`.
