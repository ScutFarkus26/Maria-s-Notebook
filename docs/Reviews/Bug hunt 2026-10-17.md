# Bug hunt 2026-10-17

> **Not fixed yet.** Found at main 5874623f.
> In short: a read-only hunt over the next-oldest code no hunt had read — every line last changed 2026-09-20 to 09-29 and never rewritten — with 48 findings (more counting the smaller items grouped under one number); the worst are a planning-calendar note deleted by clicking a day with Notes hidden, the Sample Class ticking Danny's real Reminders, Siri's "here" erasing a child's early pickup, deleting a child deleting a group meeting booking, and iPhone note photos showing sideways.

"Confirmed" means the code path was traced end to end. "Plausible" means the path is real but the trigger depends on timing, data, or something Apple doesn't document.

## Scope

Line ages were measured fresh at 5874623f with `git blame -w -M` (every line of the 1,740 source files in `Cosmic Daybook/`). Every line written before 2026-09-20 was hunted on 10-10, 10-11 and 10-12. In scope: about 25,000 lines in 752 files last changed 2026-09-20 to 09-29 and never rewritten since — the 09-21 refactor campaign, the 09-22..27 battery and Energy Fifty passes, iCloud files and albums, the Production move's app-side pieces, Siri attendance, mark times (schema 10), the Sample Class, Handoff and the pie menu.

Left out because they had their own hunts: sync and sharing, the classroom members sheet (10-05), persistence, the data model, backups and the launch dedup merges (10-05), Orders/Restock (10-09), and the Daybook Assistant (10-04). Nothing here repeats an earlier report. Six reviewers took one area each. The top findings were checked again against the code by hand before this was written: the hidden calendar note (#8), Sample Class reminders (#30), Siri "here" on a Left Early child (#1), deleting a child and group bookings (#39), the dropped-PDF title (#20), sideways photos (#21), `set_school_days` (#10), the presentation history (#45) and the Progress dashboard (#46).

## What runs through all of it

- **A live fetch swapped for a narrower signal misses edits.** The 09-26 energy work replaced whole-table `@FetchRequest`s with change counts and change flags. Where the count doesn't move, nothing redraws: the presentation history (#45), the Progress dashboard (#46), practice lists (#48), Today's "need a lesson" card (#41).
- **Older code that never learned a newer rule.** Siri's "here" predates Back in Class (#1). The Reminders mirror wasn't rekeyed when the calendar mirror was (#13); the calendar sync never got the Reminders pause (#12). `set_school_days` still matches the exact midnight that 10-11 #42 fixed for the grid (#10).
- **The Sample Class reaching the real notebook.** Reminders and Calendar (#30), a classroom switch wiping open windows (#31), Handoff of sample children and weekend sample attendance (#38).
- **Files that aren't on the device yet.** Opening a PDF on iPhone and iPad (#22), albums kept in iCloud (#18, #19), Describe Photo (#28), Share (#29).
- **Duplicate rows counted twice.** The attendance log (#2) and MCP `school_calendar` (#14) read every CloudKit copy where the rest of the app picks one.

## Attendance, Siri and the log

1. **"Mark Maya here" on a child marked Left Early erases her day: arrival becomes now, the trip is lost, and after Close Arrival an on-time child turns Late.** Confirmed (by hand), medium-high. `Siri/SiriAttendance.swift:190-199`, `Attendance/Store/CDAttendanceStore.swift:97-121`. Maya arrives 8:02, leaves for the dentist 10:15, comes back 12:40; "Mark Maya here". `markHere` special-cases only a child already present, so it marks her present (or late once arrival has closed). `mark` sets `markedAt` to 12:40 and clears `leftAt`, `returnedAt` and `statusBeforeLeavingRaw`. She is now "Late, 12:40", and the tardy goes to the front desk and ParentLocker. A tap on the grid does nothing for a Left Early tile; Back in Class (`markBack`) keeps her arrival and the trip. The Assistant shares this file. *Fix:* for a Left Early child, `markHere` calls `store.markBack(record)` (with the `before` snapshot so Undo works) and says "Maya is back in class."
2. **The attendance log lists every CloudKit copy of a child's day, so she can show twice on one day and both count.** Confirmed path, medium. `Logs/AttendanceLogFilter.swift:88-101, 126-141`, `Logs/AttendanceLogView.swift:135-149`. Every other reader uses `deduplicatedPerStudentDay()`. Two devices marking before syncing give "Present" and "Absent" rows under one day. Delete removes only one copy (`AttendanceLogView+Rows.swift:118-125`), so the other's mark comes back on the grid. *Fix:* dedupe before filtering; Delete clears every copy, as `unmark` does.
3. **The log names children by their start dates, not their records: a child entered late shows as "Unknown Student", and hidden test students still count.** Confirmed path, medium-low. `Logs/AttendanceLogFilter.swift:59-70, 116-123`, `AttendanceLogView.swift:130-142`, `+Rows.swift:23-30`. The name lookup is the roster narrowed by date and with test students removed; the records aren't. Start dates were typed in late (29 Nov / 9 Dec 2025), so fall rows read "Unknown Student" and can't be searched. *Fix:* names from the whole roster; drop hidden test students' records before counting.
4. **Siri takes a sound-alike current child over an exact match on one who has left, and a single near-miss is taken as the child.** Plausible, low-medium. `Siri/StudentAppEntity.swift:108-116`, `Siri/StudentNameMatcher.swift:44-48, 78-96`. With Leah (left) and Lea (current), "Mark Leah here" marks Lea; "Ari" heard for an "Avi" returns one result and Siri doesn't ask. The two Ettys and two Sarahs are fine. *Fix:* exact and prefix tiers over current and former children before any near-miss; no near-miss on a key of three letters or fewer.
5. **An absence reason stays on the record through later changes and comes back on a later plain Absent.** Plausible, low. `Attendance/Store/CDAttendanceStore.swift:97-121`, `Siri/SiriAttendance.swift:153-154, 178`. Vacation marked ahead, she shows up, is sent home and said absent by voice: the log, tile and ParentLocker say "Vacation". *Fix:* clear the reason whenever the status leaves Absent.
6. **The Mac Insights column counts only children enrolled on the selected day, test students included, whatever the timeframe.** Confirmed, low. `Attendance/Views/AttendanceMacView.swift:16-20`, `Attendance/Insights/AttendanceInsightsService+Sidebar.swift:95-109`. On 90-day or Year, a child who left last week drops out of the rate and Patterns. *Fix:* children active anywhere in the range, through `TestStudentsFilter.filterVisible`.
7. **Smaller.** Left Early rings the next arrival bell and plays the birthday sparkles or welcome wave as she goes home (`Attendance/Tile/AttendanceTile+Effects.swift:23-29`); Feb 29 birthdays show the cake on Feb 28 but the roster counts to Mar 1 (`Attendance/Delight/AttendanceBirthday.swift:60-67`, `Students/Roster/RosterBirthday.swift:7-17`); the log's count strip says "Tardy" where everything else says "Late" (`Logs/AttendanceLogView+Summary.swift:15`); "Mark Maya absent, sick" on a child already absent and sick answers as if it changed something (`Siri/AttendanceIntents.swift:126-130`).

## Calendars, school days, Ask AI and MCP

8. **With the Notes layer hidden, clicking a day on the planning calendar deletes or overwrites that day's note.** Confirmed (by hand), medium (data loss). `PerpetualCalendar/PlanningCalendarView.swift:134, 147, 201-206`, `PerpetualCalendar/CalendarNoteEditor.swift:49-55`. With Notes hidden the field opens empty (or with the holiday name); when focus leaves, `commit` finds the hidden note in `notesLookup` and deletes it (empty or holiday text) or replaces it with what was typed. Since 10-11 #41 a click and a click elsewhere is enough. *Fix:* open the field with the stored note whatever the layer shows; write nothing when the draft is unchanged.
9. **An Ask AI request that records something can be filed twice.** Plausible, medium. `Services/AI/NotebookTools+Meetings.swift:83-100, 125-143, 161-171`, `Services/AI/AIClientRouter.swift:205-208, 244-256`, `Chat/ChatViewModel.swift:153-162`. "Record a meeting with Maya S…": the tool saves the meeting and its goals, then the answer overflows the on-device context or hits a guardrail. With Private Cloud off, chat shows an error and puts the message back; sending it again files a second meeting. With it on, the fallback model has no tools and may say it can't record meetings. *Fix:* remember that a write tool ran; on failure say "The meeting was recorded, but the answer couldn't be finished" and keep the exchange.
10. **`set_school_days` matches a day only at exact midnight, so a day off stored at another hour can't be turned back on, and the reply says it already was.** Confirmed (by hand), medium-low. `AppCore/SchoolCalendarService+ExplicitSet.swift:47-53`. Every reader and `toggleNonSchoolDay` (10-11 #42) match the whole day. `in_session: true` replies "Already in session" while every screen shows the day off; `false` adds a second row. *Fix:* the whole-day predicate.
11. **New Chat while an answer is streaming can leave the old answer in the new chat.** Plausible, low-medium. `Chat/ChatViewModel.swift:135-143`, `Chat/StreamingTextThrottle.swift:76-81`. The throttle's update doesn't check the chat generation and outlives the cancel; it can also overwrite the next answer. *Fix:* check the generation in the publish closure; cancel the throttle on the stale return.
12. **A chosen Mac calendar that's gone makes every Calendar change re-run a failing sync, all day.** Plausible, low-medium. `Services/Calendar/CalendarSyncService.swift:173-175, 310-326`. `calendarNotFound` is thrown before the throttle's time is set, so each EventKit change adds two Sync History rows and a fresh error on Today. Reminders got a pause for this on 09-25; calendars didn't. *Fix:* the same pause.
13. **The Reminders mirror keys rows by this device's identifiers, so a second device adds its own copy of every reminder that nothing removes.** Plausible (Reminders sync on two devices), low. `Services/Calendar/EventKitMirror.swift:35-67`, `ReminderSyncService+Sync.swift:89-91`. The calendar mirror was rekeyed on 10-10 for exactly this. *Fix:* key by external identifier and the list by account plus title, adopting old rows once.
14. **MCP `school_calendar` counts a day marked twice as two days and splits a break into overlapping runs.** Confirmed, low. `Services/MCPServer/MCPNotebookTools+SchoolCalendar.swift:152-160, 189-203`. *Fix:* normalize to the day and dedupe (keeping a non-empty reason) before counting.
15. **On-device JSON answers wrapped in a code fence fail as unreadable.** Plausible, low-medium. `Services/AI/FoundationModelClient.swift:81-84`. Parsha suggestions, lesson planning, insights and story matching error out with Private Cloud off. *Fix:* strip fences and text outside the outermost braces first.
16. **Smaller.** `set_school_days` on a weekend says it saved a reason it stores nowhere (`SchoolCalendarService+ExplicitSet.swift:69-72`); `update_order_items` reports "Updated N" and is journaled when nothing changed (`MCPNotebookTools+OrderWrites.swift:201-217`); `FoundationModelClient` ignores the caller's timeout (`:106-137`); Parsha pages say Apple Intelligence "isn't available on this device" when it's only off or downloading (`Parsha/ParshaSuggestionsDetailView.swift:92-95`, `ThisWeeksParshaView.swift:346`).

## Albums, iCloud files and photos

17. **A photo picked just before Save in Quick Note is left off the note, and its file stays in iCloud for good.** Plausible (timing), medium. `Notes/QuickCapture/QuickNoteViewModel+Photo.swift:11-49`, `QuickNoteSheet.swift:325-327, 421-423`. Save is enabled on text alone; an iCloud Photos original takes seconds; the note saves without it and the late photo's cleanup is skipped because the note already saved. *Fix:* disable Save while a photo is loading; delete a photo that arrives after the save.
18. **Albums another device keeps in iCloud don't appear until a relaunch, and a device with only iCloud albums can sit on "Choose the folder".** Confirmed, medium. `Albums/Library/AlbumLibrary+ICloudShelf.swift:16-26`, `AlbumLibrary.swift:87-96, 277-294`. The shelf joins the watched folders only if it had albums at load, and a device that started on "Choose the folder" never tries again. *Fix:* refresh checks the shelf too; retry the bootstrap on activation when the shelf has albums.
19. **Albums can stay on "Opening albums…" for the session when every shelf album has been evicted.** Plausible, medium. `Albums/Library/AlbumLibrary.swift:239-263`, `AlbumLibrary+ICloudShelf.swift:101-120`. When the downloads fail (offline), the error is shown only if the old album list is empty, which it isn't. *Fix:* clear the list when only pending; show the failure when nothing arrived.
20. **A PDF dropped onto Stories or Book Club gets a random code as its title and file name.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Components/PDFLibraryListView.swift:148-153, 182-192`. The drop is copied to `tmp/<UUID>.pdf` and imported from there; the temp copy is never removed. Resources does it right (`ResourceLibraryView+Actions.swift:115-131`). *Fix:* copy into a UUID folder under the original name; remove it after.
21. **iPhone note photos show sideways in rows, and since photos sync, on the Mac too.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Services/Photos/PhotoStorageService.swift:214-248`, `Components/CachedPhotoLoader.swift:93-98`. iPhone saves keep the pixels with an orientation tag (`PhotoImageIO.swift:81-88`); the thumbnail is made without applying it and drawn as upright. The editor's preview applies it, so it looks right there. Apple Intelligence gets the sideways image too. *Fix:* `kCGImageSourceCreateThumbnailWithTransform` in both, and a new disk-cache key so the sideways copies aren't reused.
22. **Open PDF (Book Club, Stories), opening a Student File, and Open in Pages do nothing on iPhone and iPad.** Plausible, medium. `BookClub/Packets/BookClubOpenPDFButton.swift:46-58`, `Stories/StoryDetailView.swift:561-573`, `Students/Files/StudentFilesTab+FileOperations.swift:17-25`, `Lessons/Detail/LessonDetailView+FileHandling.swift:134-143`. `UIApplication.open` won't open a `file://` URL; the failure is ignored. *Fix:* Quick Look or a share sheet, as `ResourceDocumentActions.open` does.
23. **A failed "Keep in iCloud" copy can leave a broken PDF on the shelf that hides the real album on every device.** Plausible, low-medium. `Albums/Library/AlbumICloudShelf.swift:84-89`, `Utils/Files/UbiquitousFile.swift:164-180`. *Fix:* copy to a hidden name and move into place; delete on error.
24. **"Remove from iCloud" promises a copy will take the album's place even when the shelf holds the only one.** Confirmed, low-medium. `Albums/AlbumsLibraryView.swift:226-237`, `AlbumLibrary+ICloudShelf.swift:74-83`. On iPhone and iPad there's usually no chosen folder; the delete removes the only copy everywhere. *Fix:* check for another copy; otherwise say so, or move it to the Trash.
25. **After "Keep in iCloud", changes to the original album PDF are ignored.** Confirmed, low-medium. `Albums/Library/AlbumLibrary.swift:235-252`. The shelf copy wins the name and the "Updated" badge watches only it. *Fix:* notice a newer copy in a chosen folder and offer to replace.
26. **Open PDF stays greyed out, and a Student File card keeps its blank icon, when the file arrives after the screen opened.** Plausible, low. `Utils/Files/DocumentFileURLMemo.swift:40-51`, `Students/Files/PDFThumbnailView.swift:54-56`. *Fix:* don't remember "no file"; `.task(id: url)`.
27. **Related Lessons and "by meaning" search keep vectors for albums that have left the library.** Plausible, low. `Albums/Search/AlbumSemanticIndex.swift:73-97`. A renamed album's old copy takes Related Lessons slots. *Fix:* build fresh and swap.
28. **Describe Photo and tag suggestions can't see a note photo that isn't downloaded.** Plausible, low. `PhotoStorageService.swift:235-248`, `Notes/Editor/NoteEditorAISuggestion.swift:48-52`. *Fix:* download first, then load off the main thread.
29. **Smaller.** Album bookmarks start security-scoped access on every load and never stop (`AlbumLibrary.swift:227-231`); renaming a lesson attachment can take the name of another device's not-yet-downloaded file (`Lessons/Attachments/LessonFileStorage.swift:266-294`); a note given a new photo keeps showing the old one until the row rebuilds (`Components/AsyncCachedImage.swift:84-106`); quitting the Mac within 1.5 s of an album page turn loses the reading position (`Albums/Detail/AlbumDetailView.swift:130-142, 192-198`); resource Share offers a file that isn't downloaded (`Resources/ResourceFileStorage.swift:64-71`).

## App shell, windows and the Sample Class

30. **Opening Today in the Sample Class copies Danny's real Reminders and Calendar into it, and ticking a reminder there ticks it in Apple Reminders.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `AppCore/AppDependencies.swift:105-128`, `Today/Views/TodayView.swift:303-307, 374-402`. Reading `dependencies.reminderSync` points the one shared service at whichever store asked, so the Sample Class's Today syncs the real list into the practice store, and a tick there writes the real reminder (which My Class then marks done). Back in My Class, EventKit changes keep landing in the sample store until a My Class screen reads the service, and the sample sync's time skips My Class's next sync for 10 minutes. *Fix:* never rebind for the Sample Class; Today's syncs and write-back return early there.
31. **Switching classroom rebuilds every window, so an observation or sheet being typed anywhere is lost without asking.** Confirmed, medium. `AppCore/ActiveClassroomEnvironmentModifier.swift:18`, on the main window and every detail window (`Windows/DetailWindowScene.swift:122`). An observation open in its own window shows "Observation Not Found"; the text is gone on return. *Fix:* ask before switching while an editor or sheet is open (or disable the switch then); detail windows close instead of rebuilding.
32. **A Handoff that launches the app is probably dropped.** Plausible, medium. `AppCore/CosmicDaybookApp+MainWindow.swift:69`, `AppCore/Handoff.swift:96-116`. The handler exists only once the notebook has opened; the activity arrives during "Opening your notebook…". *Fix:* attach it to the window content that always exists; the router already holds a request until the screen is ready.
33. **On the Mac, each Handoff probably opens an extra main window.** Plausible, low. `Handoff.swift:96-116`. Nothing declares `handlesExternalEvents`. *Fix:* declare it on the main window.
34. **A tap on the floating + that drifts 2 points or more moves the button instead of opening quick capture.** Confirmed path, low. `AppCore/RootView/QuickNoteGlassButton.swift:226-273`. *Fix:* count anything under the 10-point long-press slop as a tap.
35. **The MCP tools' day formatter keeps the time zone the app started in.** Plausible, low. `Utils/Formatting/DateFormatters.swift:179-186` (as `MCPNotebookTools.isoDay`). With the Mac open across a trip west, "2026-10-17" lands on the 16th. *Fix:* per-zone, like `isoDateLocal(in:)`.
36. **With no main window open, "open in new window" and Help › Keyboard Shortcuts do nothing.** Confirmed, low. `AppCore/Windows/OpenWindowOnNotificationModifier.swift:16-45`, `Windows/MainWindowRegistry.swift:41-46`. *Fix:* route through `MainWindowOpener`.
37. **Lessons & Work's New Work goes through a flag every main window watches.** Already fixed (plan review): 10-11 #5 made `WorksAgendaView+Chrome.swift:176` call the window's own `quickCapture.newWork()`; the flag is only the fallback for a window outside RootView. Was: plausible, low. `AppCore/RootView/RootView.swift:228-232`. With two Mac windows, the wrong one (or both) opens the sheet. *Fix:* the window's own `quickCaptureActions?.newWork()`.
38. **Smaller.** A cancelled press can open the pie menu with no finger down (`QuickNoteGlassButton.swift:213-302`); a second toast is cut short by the first's timer (`Components/Toast/ToastBanner.swift:43-58`); the Sample Class seeds weekend attendance and plans on days off (`SampleClassroomSeeder+FakeRecords.swift:89-109, 161-171`); Handoff advertises sample children, which open "not found" elsewhere (`Handoff.swift:46-48`).

## Students, Today and settings

39. **Deleting a child deletes a group meeting booking for everyone in it if she was its first participant; otherwise she stays in it as a ghost.** Confirmed (by hand), medium. `Students/Services/StudentDeletion.swift:62-79, 93-98`, `Students/Meetings/Workflow/MeetingScheduler.swift:144-146, 179-195`. `ScheduledMeeting` is deleted by `studentID`, which a group booking sets to its first participant — against the delete alert's "anything shared with other children stays for those children". When she isn't first, her id stays and the booking is never cleaned up. *Fix:* drop her from group bookings (deleting one only when she was its last participant), using `MeetingScheduler.remove`'s rule without saving.
40. **A child put back in the Meetings queue by hand never leaves Up Next once her meeting is filed through Claude.** Confirmed path, low-medium. `Students/Meetings/Workflow/MeetingQueueRequeueStore.swift:32-47`. The requeue is stamped 10:15; `create_meeting_entry` stores the meeting at midnight, which reads as earlier. *Fix:* end the requeue when a new meeting row appears, not by clock time.
41. **Today's "N children need a lesson" card doesn't change after a lesson is given while Today stays open.** Confirmed, low. `Today/Views/TodayView.swift:300-325, 360-370`, `TodayViewModel+DerivedCounts.swift:56-74`. The Mac sits on Today all day. *Fix:* recount at the end of `reload()` (its change flag skips the work when nothing moved).
42. **A meeting- or note-template delete that fails to save looks done and stays pending.** Confirmed, low. `Settings/Templates/MeetingTemplateManagementView.swift:39-47`, `NoteTemplateManagementView.swift:28-35`. *Fix:* `SaveCoordinator.saveEdit` and the save-failed alert.
43. **A spreadsheet with a blank first line loses its header row.** Plausible, low. `Students/Import/CSVUtils.swift:101-107, 146-153`. The header can import as a child named "First Name Last Name". *Fix:* drop all-blank rows before finding the header.
44. **Smaller.** "School days since last lesson" on the record and the roster's lesson age use different rules (`Students/Detail/DaysSinceLastLessonView.swift:57-77`); deleting a child leaves her id on issue actions and her deleted work's ids in practice sessions (`StudentDeletion.swift:208-233`); deleting a project leaves its old template-week rows, which backups carry (`Projects/Services/ProjectDeletion.swift:51-67`); the meeting-template editor requires all five prompts, which the built-in Quick Check-In doesn't have (`MeetingTemplateEditorSheet.swift:32-38`); the filter chip says "1 Students" when the chosen child is gone (`Components/MultiSelectFilterMenu.swift:36-42`).

## Lessons, presentations and work

45. **On the Mac, the presentation history doesn't update when a given presentation is edited.** Confirmed (by hand), medium-low. `Presentations/Assignments/LessonAssignmentHistoryView.swift:236-246`, `+ChangeDetection.swift:18-25`. Since a9b3c09b it reloads only when the number of given presentations changes. Moving one from Tuesday to Monday, adding a child, an MCP re-date or a synced edit leaves it under Tuesday with the old names. iPhone and iPad redraw by accident when the sheet closes. *Fix:* reload on any change to a given presentation; keep the count only for deciding whether to refetch.
46. **The Progress dashboard doesn't refresh when a lesson is marked mastered or a lesson is edited.** Confirmed (by hand), low-medium. `ProgressDashboard/ProgressDashboardView.swift:27-29`. Since 10-10 #34 "Mastered" comes from `CDLessonPresentation`, but the screen watches only `LessonAssignment` and `WorkModel`, so `mark_mastered`, a checklist mark, a synced mark or a lesson rename leave pills stale. *Fix:* also watch `LessonPresentation` and `Lesson`.
47. **Dropping several selected presentations onto a day gives them all one time and loses their order.** Confirmed, low. `Presentations/Planning/WeekDayColumnDropDelegate.swift:38-56, 77-125`. Each is placed against the column as it was drawn, so all three land at 9:00:01. *Fix:* place the batch in one pass and save once.
48. **Smaller.** Work detail's and the presentation sheet's practice lists miss a session edited elsewhere that newly adds this work or child (`Work/Detail/WorkDetailView.swift:110-116`, `LessonAssignmentDetailSheet.swift:178-184`); a batch "Mark Presented" goes through the toggle and can take a child off a presentation recorded on another device since the grid refreshed (`Lessons/Checklist/Model/ChecklistBatchActionExecutor.swift:47-56, 185-193`).
