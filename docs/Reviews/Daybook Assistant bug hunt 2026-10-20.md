# Daybook Assistant bug hunt 2026-10-20

> **Not fixed yet.** Found at main 884cf9bd; the fixes are planned in <../Plans/Plan - Daybook Assistant bug hunt 2026-10-20 fixes.md>.
> In short: a read-only hunt over the whole Daybook Assistant (its own ~12,600 lines plus how it uses the ~22,400 shared notebook lines it compiles) found 28 findings plus five groups of smaller ones, and no iOS 18 problems; the worst are the grid opening on yesterday the next morning, the Late phase slipping back to Arrival on phones that didn't close arrival, a stray tap losing "Absent, Sick", one stuck send holding every later mark on the phone, and a reminder tap emailing the front desk a second time.

"Confirmed" means the code path was traced end to end. "Plausible" means the path is real but the trigger depends on timing, data, or something Apple doesn't document.

## Scope

Everything compiled into the "Daybook Assistant" target at 884cf9bd: `Daybook Assistant/` (89 files) and the 134 shared files from `Cosmic Daybook/` (attendance store, tiles and rules, front-desk email, ParentLocker, sharing, sync, Siri, Restock service). The shared code was read for how the Assistant reaches it; its own internals had hunts of their own (sync and sharing 10-05, ParentLocker twice on 10-13, Restock 10-09, Siri 10-17). Five Opus reviewers took one area each: the attendance grid, sync and joining, the front desk and reminders, Siri and Restock, and the app shell with onboarding, the Sample Class and iOS 18. Nothing repeats an earlier report (10-04 hunt, the 20 fixes, the 10-07 battery check, 10-16); every earlier fix the reviewers traced still works.

Checked by hand before this was written: #1, #2, #7, #15, #17.

**iOS 18 (an assistant's SE runs 18.6.2): clean.** Three version checks in the whole target, all with correct fallbacks; every SF Symbol exists on iOS 17; Siri phrases work under the Home Screen name; `MeshGradient`, `Tab` and `onGeometryChange` are iOS 18.0.

## What runs through all of it

- **A phone's view of the day built from one signal that later edits erase.** Late is known on other phones only from Close Arrival's automatic absences (#2, #5); a send is judged by the newest one (#15); the day on screen is moved on only by a scene-phase change the tab may not hear (#1).
- **Acting on the phone's copy before iCloud has caught up.** The front-desk reminder tap (#14), joining over a rebuild (#8), leaving with marks not yet uploaded (#12).
- **Waits with no end.** A share call that never returns (#7), a ParentLocker send that gave up while offline (#16), a backlog stranded by a hold.

## Attendance grid

1. **The next morning the grid can open on yesterday, and her taps change yesterday's marks.** Plausible, high. `Daybook Assistant/Attendance/AssistantAttendanceView.swift:218-228`, `Daybook Assistant/Sync/AssistantReloadOnReturn.swift:23-41`. Left on Restock overnight, the Attendance tab's `.task` reloads the day on show (Monday); only the scene-phase handler moves to today, and it sits on a tab that may not hear the app come back. Left on another day, the handler keeps that day on purpose. Either way Tuesday's taps change Monday. *Fix:* whenever the screen reloads after being away (tab or app), if the calendar day changed since she was last there, go to today.
2. **On a phone that didn't close arrival, Late slips back to Arrival once the automatic absences are gone, and a late child is marked Present.** Confirmed, medium. `AssistantAttendanceViewModel.swift:243-245`, `Cosmic Daybook/Attendance/Rules/AttendanceLatePhase.swift:38-44`, `CDAttendanceStore.swift:104-108`. The guide closes on the Mac; Maya is tapped Late and Ari given "Sick"; no automatic absence is left, so her phone reads Arrival and Ari arriving at 10:30 is Present (front desk, ParentLocker). Same rule in Siri's "here" and the notebook. *Fix:* a device that has seen the day closed remembers it, and forgets only when the automatic absences go back to unmarked (an Undo), not when they're resolved.
3. **A stray tap on a child marked "Absent, Sick" loses the absence and the reason, and a second tap goes to Unmarked.** Confirmed, medium. `Cosmic Daybook/Attendance/Rules/AttendanceRules.swift:26-27`, `AttendanceRecordEntity.swift:72-77`. *Fix:* keep the tile's mark from before her last tap; the next tap on that tile restores it exactly (`CDAttendanceStore.revert`).
4. **"Mark N Absent & Email" opens the email even when closing arrival failed, leaving the unmarked children out of it.** Confirmed, low. `AssistantArrivalBar.swift:154-157`, `AssistantAttendanceViewModel.swift:316-329`. *Fix:* open the email only when the close went through; otherwise show the save error.
5. **Two phones close arrival, one taps Undo: the other stays in Late with everyone unmarked, so on-time arrivals are marked Late.** Plausible, low. `AttendanceLatePhase.swift:41`. *Fix:* with #2.
6. **Smaller.** A VoiceOver double-tap on a tile where a tap does nothing clears the Close Arrival Undo (`AssistantAttendanceView.swift:405-409`); going back to Late with nobody unmarked queues a ParentLocker send with no Undo (`AssistantArrivalBar.swift:333-345`); the First Day header waits for a reload (`AssistantAttendanceViewModel.swift:269-274`); VoiceOver says "tardy" where the screen says "Late" (`AttendanceTile+Menu.swift:78`); days off, day locks, records and the reminders read both stores while the roll, Siri and ParentLocker read only the class share (`AssistantDayRoll.swift:50-55`, `ArrivalReminder.swift:110`, `FrontDeskEmailReminder.swift:150`, `AssistantAttendanceViewModel+Rules.swift:72`). Not changing: a Clear Mark lost to an earlier mark that syncs later (the duplicate rule prefers a marked copy, `AttendanceDeduplication.swift:35`).

## Sync and joining

7. **One send that never finishes holds every later mark on the phone until she quits the app, and the line says "Sending to iCloud…".** Plausible, medium. `Daybook Assistant/Sync/AssistantShareAttacher.swift:162-169, 225` → `Cosmic Daybook/Sharing/ClassroomShareAttach.swift:294-306`; `AssistantBootstrapper+Class.swift:118-125`. The share call has no time limit (the notebook has `ClassroomShareAttachLock`), and a rebuilt stack still waits on the old pass. *Fix:* time each pass; past the limit, show "Marks aren't sending. Quit the app and open it again." and let a rebuilt stack start a new pass.
8. **Opening the invitation just after signing in to iCloud can lose the join.** Plausible, medium. `AssistantBootstrapper.swift:178-194, 311-337`, `ClassroomSharingService.swift:211-245`. The account turning available rebuilds the stack while the join runs on the old one; she stays on "Waiting for the invitation…". *Fix:* `rebuildStack` waits while a join is running (up to its one-minute limit), then rebuilds.
9. **The rebuild after a late iCloud sign-in is decided from the account status alone, so it can be skipped when it's needed.** Plausible, medium-low. `AssistantBootstrapper+AccountDecision.swift:18-26`, `AssistantFilingHold.swift:68-81`. Marks then wait for good with no message. *Fix:* decide from the class store's own failed setup: failed and the account now available means rebuild once.
10. **Joining and Leave read the classroom share on the main thread while the first big download runs, freezing the screen.** Plausible, low-medium. `ClassroomSharingService.swift:135-142, 239, 277-280`. *Fix:* `refreshParticipantsOffMain()` in the join; `ClassroomShareAttach.shares(inStoreWithIdentifier:)` in Leave.
11. **Accepting a second class's invitation while in one mixes the two, and marks go to the wrong guide.** Confirmed path, low-medium (rare). `ClassroomSharingService.swift:221-231, 279-284`, `AssistantDayRoll.swift:48-55`. *Fix:* refuse with "Leave your current class first."
12. **After a relaunch, Leave doesn't warn about marks that went into the share but hadn't uploaded.** Confirmed, low. `AssistantBootstrapper+Class.swift:152-157`, `UnsentChangesKeepAlive.swift:37, 115`. *Fix:* the unsent check also reads the sync line's record (last save after last upload).
13. **Smaller.** A hold cancels the retry timer and a release before CloudKit's "wait until" sets none (`AssistantShareAttacher.swift:374-392`); "Download from iCloud again" shows the join screen until the membership returns (`AssistantBootstrapper.swift:189-201`); the guide's Apple name is read once, so a membership that arrives first (or a class that arrives while the Sample Class is open, `wasReady`) leaves "Your guide" until a relaunch and skips the retry and removed checks (`AssistantBootstrapper.swift:234-244, 347-354`); a Siri command before startup that finds sync stopped has nobody listening (`AssistantBootstrapper+Class.swift:73-77`).

## Front desk, ParentLocker and reminders

14. **Tapping the front-desk reminder opens the email before the phone has caught up, so a second assistant emails the front desk again.** Plausible, medium. `Daybook Assistant/FrontDesk/AssistantFrontDeskMail.swift:27-30, 75-92`. *Fix:* after a reminder tap brings the app forward, wait for the first iCloud import (up to ~10 s, "Checking whether it's gone…"), then check for a send again.
15. **Send Again, or a second assistant's send, relabels an on-time email "(late)" and changes the sender for everyone.** Confirmed, medium-low. `AssistantFrontDeskRow.swift:170-174`, `AttendanceEmailLog+Sends.swift:41-47`, `AttendanceEmailLog.swift:213-216`. Read by the notebook and MCP too. *Fix:* on time or late, and who, from the day's first send; a resend shows as "· sent again 10:15".
16. **A ParentLocker send that gave up while offline isn't tried again when the network comes back.** Confirmed, medium-low. `ParentLockerSync+Run.swift:353-359`, `ParentLockerSync+Changes.swift:174-197`, `AssistantNetworkWatch.swift`. *Fix:* the network watch also runs the sync's return path.
17. **Every past school day with no send on record shows a prominent "Send to ParentLocker".** Confirmed, low-medium. `AssistantParentLocker.swift:17-28` → `AssistantFrontDeskRow.swift:203`; the notebook too (`AttendanceExpandedView+ParentLocker.swift:37`). Aug 25–Sep 29 predate send records; one tap sends a month-old day. *Fix:* the button only for today; other days keep Send Now in the status menu, behind a question.
18. **An old front-desk notification tapped on a later day acts on today, and delivered ones are never cleared.** Confirmed path, low. `ArrivalReminder.swift:156-166`, `FrontDeskEmailReminder.swift:66-71`. *Fix:* ignore a tap for a day that isn't today; remove a day's delivered front-desk notifications once a send is recorded or arrives.
19. **On a day ParentLocker isn't expecting attendance, the line still offers "Send to ParentLocker".** Confirmed, low. `AssistantParentLocker.swift:25-28`. *Fix:* add `!isNotExpected(on:)`.
20. **Smaller.** Leaving the class can bring its reminders back (`EarlyPickupReminderUpkeep.swift:89-101`; check membership before each rebuild); "Not Now" on setup's reminder page doesn't stop the permission prompt (`ArrivalReminder.swift:232-247`); a reminder tapped while a past day's email is open records the send on today (`AssistantFrontDesk.swift:91`); a failed send in Mail says nothing (`AssistantFrontDeskMail.swift:38`). Not changing: reminders ring on summer weekdays unless summer is entered as days off.

## Onboarding and the Sample Class

21. **Tapping the invitation while the Sample Class is open shows no sign of joining, and a cold launch reopens the sample over a running join.** Confirmed, low-medium. `AssistantBootstrapper.swift:117, 130, 347-372`, `AssistantRootView.swift:40-48`. *Fix:* an arriving invitation leaves the sample; don't reopen it while joining.
22. **Coming back from signing in to iCloud throws setup back to Welcome.** Confirmed, low-medium. `AssistantOnboardingView.swift:23, 40-42`, `AssistantRootView.swift:34-35`. *Fix:* keep the page across the rebuild, or don't show "Starting…" for a rebuild while the join screen is up.
23. **A pickup set in the Sample Class never asks for notifications, so it never rings.** Confirmed, low. `ArrivalReminder.swift:238`. *Fix:* the sample may ask too.
24. **Notifications refused in setup, and the summary still says "Arrival reminder 8:15 AM".** Confirmed, low. `AssistantSetupFlow.swift:237-246`, `AssistantSetupPages.swift:180-183`. *Fix:* read the permission; say so with a Settings link, and "Notifications off" on the summary.
25. **Smaller.** An undetermined iCloud status reads "Signed in to iCloud…" and a failed first check spins until the app returns (`AssistantIntroPages.swift:186-188`); setup's name field is a fixed 54 pt and clips at the largest text (`AssistantSetupFlow.swift:98-106`); leaving the sample mid-update can leave its pickup reminders (`EarlyPickupReminder.swift:186-190`); two name sheets can both open after an Apple Account change (`AssistantTabs.swift:57-59`).

## Siri and Restock

26. **Siri can mark the wrong child when two names sound the same but are spelled differently (Sara and Sarah).** Plausible, medium. `Cosmic Daybook/Siri/StudentNameMatcher.swift:35-48`, `AttendanceGridNames.swift:13-28`. The exact tier stops at the spelling the recognizer wrote; the grid and Siri's answer don't tell them apart. *Fix:* the exact tier also returns names that sound identical (a final "h" folded), so Siri asks; those children get an initial.
27. **Two devices opening a need for the same staple can lose the quantity the assistant asked for.** Plausible, low-medium. `RestockService+Needs.swift:263-303`. *Fix:* when the kept need hasn't been asked for, keep the larger quantity and any note.
28. **"We're out of X" says "already marked out" and leaves nothing on the office run when the staple lost its need.** Plausible, low. `AssistantSiriRestock.swift:98-102, 117-118`. *Fix:* call `setLevel` even when the level matches.
29. **On a locked phone Siri can say a former child's full name.** Plausible, low. `SiriAttendance.swift:95-105`. *Fix:* the grid name; in the Assistant, answer as not found.
30. **Tapping a staple the guide just deleted puts an orphan item on the office run.** Plausible, low. `AssistantRestockModel.swift:200-213`. *Fix:* `tap`, `setLevel`, `setNote` do nothing and reload when the staple is gone.
31. **Restock history names nobody for an assistant with no name set.** Confirmed, low. `RestockModels.swift:174-179`, `AssistantRestockHistorySheet.swift:47-51`. *Fix:* fall back to `RestockAuthor.reads(changedByID:name:)`.
32. **Siri's "Add to the office run" keeps ending punctuation, so "Glue sticks." doubles.** Plausible, low. `AssistantSiriRestock.swift:113-114`. *Fix:* `AssistantRestockModel.entry(_:)` first.
33. **Smaller.** A stale "Couldn't save" banner after a later save (`AssistantRestockModel.swift:357-381`); "We need…" on a one-off already listed ignores the new quantity (`RestockService+Needs.swift:33-35`); the join screen's sample "We need…" Siri tip can't work. Not changing: Siri's staple matching in reverse ("toilet paper" → "Paper"; Siri says the name aloud), and the debug-only `-AssistantSampleClass` defaults.
