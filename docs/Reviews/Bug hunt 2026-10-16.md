## Assistant sending

1. **A retry asked for while a send is still going is dropped, so her marks wait out the whole rest even though the network is back.** Confirmed path, timing-dependent, medium. `Daybook Assistant/Sync/AssistantShareAttacher.swift:174-178, 161-164, 202-225, 285-296`. `retryWaiting()` clears the rest and calls `flush`. With a pass running, `flush` only sets `runAgain`. When that pass then fails, `afterRound` writes a new rest. The extra round finds the backlog not due and `fresh` already empty, so it tries nothing and the loop ends. It hits all three new callers: the network watch, the lock-time keep-alive and `onSharedStoreSynced` (`AssistantStack.swift:70-80`). For example, she taps on a dying link. Cellular takes over 3 s later, but the pass still waiting on the old link times out and rests 30–120 s. Locking right after a tap with no connection (the plan's own device check 3) loses its retry the same way, and the phone suspends with the mark still waiting. *Fix:* a retry that arrives during a pass sets a flag, and `run()` honors it after `afterRound` by putting `backlogWaitsUntil` back to `notBefore`.
2. **"They'll go when you're back online" stays up after she's back online, and keeps cutting the longer rest short.** Confirmed, low-medium. `AssistantShareAttacher.swift:233, 285-288`. Only a round where some mark went in clears `isOffline`. Suppose a later round is refused for another reason: CloudKit busy, a mark in the wrong share zone, or no classroom share yet. The offline line then stays wrong, and every sync with the guide (`AssistantStack.swift:77`) and every lock retries on the strength of `isOffline`, resetting the 1–10 min rest. *Fix:* in `afterRound`'s failure branch, set `isOffline = result.noConnection`.
3. **Locking the phone retries marks that are resting after refusals.** Confirmed, low. `AssistantStack.swift:70-73`, `Cosmic Daybook/Services/Sync/UnsentChangesKeepAlive.swift:185-204`. The "offline, or rest over" test is only in `hasWaitingWork`. The keep-alive also starts for any unsent save, and then `waitForWork` calls `retryWaiting()` regardless. *Fix:* in `waitForWork`, retry only when `pendingCount > 0 && (isOffline || backlogIsDue())`.

Looked clean: the share pass in both apps (untried marks kept, errors unwrapped, CloudKit's retry-after honored, `share(_:to:)` never called offline), the guide's side, the back-off numbers, the network watch (one monitor, stopped on rebuild, a 3-s settle, no idle cost), the lock-time background task, and Who's Absent.

## Lessons & Work

4. **On a weekend or holiday, Move All Forward and Check Today put work checks on a day the strip doesn't show.** Confirmed, medium. `Presentations/Planning/CalendarForwardShiftService.swift:108-118` lands every overdue check on the start of the calendar day. `WeekPlanSection+Prompts.swift:56-63` passes `Date()`. `WorkToShowFirst+Actions.swift:73-84, 102-122` (`todayCheck`, `checkToday`) also use the calendar day. The strip only has columns for school days (`WeekPlanSection+Data.swift:44-48`). Planning on Saturday, Move All Forward sends Friday's and older checks to Saturday and Monday's to Tuesday. The overdue ones then sit on no column, though the dialog says they "come to today". On a weekend, a chip's "Move Monday's Check to Today" pulls Monday's check back to Saturday. *Fix:* use the first school day on or after today in both places.
5. **Check Today on a shared row moves the check for every child on it.** Confirmed, low-medium. The chip popover offers it on every row (`WorkToShowFirstPopover.swift:65-73`), and `checkToday` never asks whether the row is hers alone. Take Ada's chip on shared project or book-club work: "Move Thursday's Check to Today" moves the group's one check to today, and nothing says it's shared. The Phase 6 review stopped Before you start from logging shared rows for this reason, but missed this button. *Fix:* shared rows offer Open Work only, as Before you start does.
6. **Moving a child to another group leaves her own notes behind.** Confirmed path, low-medium (only when a group has a note about her alone). `Presentations/Services/PresentationGroupMove.swift:96-120` uses `PresentationRecordCleanup.moveStudents` (`PresentationRecordCleanup.swift:91-103`), which moves year-plan entries but not notes. Notes move only when her old group ends up empty. Dragging her chip goes through the same code. *Fix:* move any note on the old group that is about only her; Undo goes through the same code.

Looked clean: no check moves twice or lands on a day its work already has one, linked copies stay together, the dialog's counts match, Undo of Move All Forward and Clear All (failed saves included), Clear All keeping noted checks as Skipped, the three-day window, group-move Undo, her-own-copy vs shared-row rules, and the Before a Lesson pill.

## Fixes

All six were fixed in dd67b3e5. Each fix has a test that was run first and failed without it. Focused suites pass: notebook 87 tests in 8 suites, Assistant 80 in 7. The iOS, Mac and Assistant builds are clean.

- #1: a `retryWaiting()` during a round sets a flag, and a failed round with it set goes again at once (`AssistantShareAttacher.run`).
- #2: a failed round sets `isOffline` to whether it had no connection.
- #3: the lock-time keep-alive calls `retryWaitingIfAChance()`. `waitingHaveAChance` is the one test both closures use.
- #4: Move All Forward's overdue landing is the first school day from today. Check Today uses `WorkToShowFirst.checkDay` and names that day ("Check Monday", "Added to Monday's checks"). The dialog says "the next school day on a weekend or holiday". `ReviewFixes1012A`'s stand-in calendar now gives a real answer for Sunday's next school day.
- #5: `checkToday` refuses a row others are on (`.sharedWithOthers`). The popover marks it "Shared work" and offers only Open Work.
- #6: `PresentationRecordCleanup.moveStudents` moves notes about only the moved children. This covers group moves, chip drags and moving children to a new presentation, and Undo brings them back.

## Review of the fixes

A code review of dd67b3e5 found nine more. Each behavior fix has a test that failed first.

- A retry during a send that then failed went again at once, but the cut-short try still counted as a failure, so the next rest doubled. The cut-short try now isn't counted or rested on (`AssistantShareAttacher.goesAgainAtOnce`).
- A round that put some marks in before the connection dropped said "offline". It now says so only when nothing reached iCloud.
- Undo of a group move took back every note on the other group about her alone, including ones it already had. The receipt now keeps the notes the move carried, and Undo takes back only those.
- This file's status line said the fixes weren't on main; they were.
- Past the coming week (after a long break) the buttons and toasts read "On Thu, Jan 7's Checks". They now read "On the Checks for Thu, Jan 7", "Check on Thu, Jan 7", and "Checked for Monday" rather than "Checked Monday".
- Move All Forward could put overdue checks on yesterday if no school day was found ahead; it never lands them before today now. Check Today's day comes from the new `SchoolCalendarService.schoolDaySync(onOrAfter:)`, which `YearPlanPacing.schoolDay(onOrAfter:)` also uses.
- The popover works out the check day once rather than once per row.
- `moveNotes(from:to:only:)` is the one loop that moves notes, and `ownNotes(of:on:)` picks a moving child's.
- The "Shared work" line is `WorkToShowFirst.Item.subtitle`, used by the popover and Before You Start.
