# Bug hunt 2026-10-29

> **All fixed** (Parts 1 and 2). Found at main afdce088.
> In short: two passes. Part 1 went over the presentation sheet: the split feature, then the older code around it (10 bugs). Part 2 was three hunts over every line changed since 2026-09-30 that no earlier hunt had read, in the areas where a mistake costs most (22 bugs). All 32 are fixed, with tests.

## Scope

Earlier hunts (10-10, 10-11, 10-12, 10-17) read every line last changed before 2026-09-30. Part 2 read the lines changed since then, in three areas:

- data, sync and Claude's tools: Backup, Sharing, Services, MCPServer, AppCore, Repositories, Models, Settings;
- the classroom: Students, Presentations, Work, Planning, Lessons, Today, Attendance, Schedules, ClassGrid;
- the rest of the app's features.

The top one or two findings in each area were checked again by hand against the code before this was written. The rest are each hunt's own traces.

## Part 1: the presentation sheet (fixed)

1. **Cancel after a split could delete a saved plan and its notes.** It happened when a split took off every child saved on the plan while another child was only added in the sheet. Now that added child is saved on the plan, and Undo takes them off again.
2. **Split Off did nothing on a new presentation with no lesson picked.** The sheet now asks for the lesson first and keeps the button off until one is picked.
3. **Going back to Edit Details lost How It Went's work.** Saving or cancelling from there lost the notes and choices, and going back into How It Went reset the choices. Now the choices stay, and closing the sheet from Edit Details saves them as Later does.
4. **Undo after a roster or lesson change left a child marked as given.** Undo is no longer offered once the record has changed after Record.
5. **Saving an old given presentation again rewrote its history.** It reset each child's last-observed date and put older children back in Following. Now a re-save leaves both alone; a corrected date still moves them, and Record still follows every child.
6. **"Earlier, Date Unknown" recorded children marked as not there.** Now they stay on a plan of their own, as Record keeps them. Cancelling its "Also remove from work?" question leaves the sheet as it was, not given.
7. **Save worked with no lesson picked.** That lost a typed note, or left a plan for a lesson that doesn't exist. Save now waits for a lesson.
8. **A Record that failed left its edits waiting** for the app's next save. Now a failure takes back everything Record did, so "Nothing was changed" is true.
9. **Change Lesson on a given presentation left its open practice on the old lesson.** The open work now moves with the presentation, and "Practice: …" titles take the new lesson's name. Finished work stays as it was.
10. **Change Lesson kept the old lesson's saved name** in note labels and history. It now updates.

Also fixed: **a pickup time 3 days ahead was an hour off when the clocks changed in between** (`AttendanceRules`). Times were worked out as minutes after midnight. Pickup times now use clock times. The suite's pickup test failed on 2026-10-29 because November 1 is a clock-change day.


## Part 2: code changed since 2026-09-30 (fixed)

### Data, sync and Claude's tools

1. **Replace restore from an older backup empties Schedules, and assistants' names, with no warning.** It affects backups older than format 42 (38 for names). `deleteAll` clears every type whatever the backup's format. (`Backup/BackupService+Helpers.swift:44`) Checked by hand.
2. **`change_schedule_day` with `block_id` ignores the date.** It changes or removes a one-off on another day, or writes a change no day reads, and replies as if it worked. (`MCPNotebookTools+TimetableWrites.swift:397`) Checked by hand.
3. **`set_timetable` drops later one-day changes without listing them in its preview.** A `from` date in the past rewrites past weeks, though the preview says past weeks keep them. (`TimetableWrites.swift:133`)
4. **A lead guide's notebook accepts another guide's invitation, and Leave then does nothing.** (`Sharing/ClassroomSharingService+Joining.swift:25`; rare)
5. **The student dedupe brings back a ParentLocker link or "born after sunset" that was cleared,** when an older copy still has it. (`DataCleanupService+DeduplicateStudents.swift:28`; plausible)
6. **Replace restore brings back an assistant's old name.** (`BackupRestoreRun+LaterTypes.swift:296`; low impact)
7. **Merge restore can leave a note with two links to one child.** (`BackupService+RestoreAftercare.swift:68`; plausible)
8. **Two devices can end up sharing one auto-backup folder and trimming each other's backups,** after a device restore copies the folder's name across. (`AutoBackupFolders.swift:39`; plausible)
9. **A to-do from Tide that was deleted here can come back,** when its Tide action couldn't be marked. (`TideSyncEngine+Steps.swift:216`; low impact)

How they were fixed, in short:

- 1: each backed-up type records the first backup format that holds it, and Replace (and its preview) leaves alone a type the backup is too old to hold. Settings now says so.
- 2: a `block_id` not on that date is refused, with a message saying which day it is on. 3: the preview lists every one-day change it drops, and says when a past `from` changes the days since.
- 4: a lead guide's notebook refuses another class's invitation and says to open it in the Daybook Assistant. 5: the copy changed last decides the ParentLocker link and "born after sunset".
- 6: Replace updates assistants' names the way Merge does. 7: a restore leaves one link per child on a note. 8: each device marks its backup folder name as its own in a Keychain item a device backup doesn't carry; folders already shared stay shared. 9: the Tide sync remembers an action it couldn't mark.

Outside this pass's lines: `BackupEncryptionKeyStore.fetchOrCreateKey` can make a second key on a new device before iCloud Keychain delivers the first. Backups made with one of the two keys then can't be opened on the other.

### The classroom

10. **Practicing or Reviewing on one child's Checklist cell can change a classmate's linked work.** It takes the first open row naming her, not her own. (`ClassSubjectChecklistViewModel+LadderSteps.swift:47`) Checked by hand.
11. **Editing a Schedules block on a day with a one-day change carries that change into later weeks** with This Day and After, or into every week. (`ScheduleBlockSheet.swift:248`)
12. **The Checklist calls a child Ready when her year plan already has the lesson, and its Plan makes a second plan.** (`ClassGridEngine+Checklist.swift:119`)
13. **A note about only an absent child stays on the presentation she missed** (Record, and Today's "Move them to tomorrow"). (`PresentationRecorder.swift:118`) Checked by hand.
14. **The block sheet rewrites fields it doesn't show,** so Save with nothing changed still marks the block changed: a room's children, an odd length, the children's order. (`ScheduleBlockSheet.swift:221`)
15. **The grid doesn't refresh for a recall check, a work-participant change or a year-plan entry synced from another device.** (`ClassGridChecklistView+RemoteChanges.swift:13`; low impact)
16. **The Children view ignores shared project work the child doesn't own.** (`ClassGridChildrenModel.swift:93`; low impact)
17. **Any room block Danny teaches counts as his adolescent-room turn.** (`ScheduleDayResolver.swift:197`; plausible)

Smaller: a block cancelled for one day can only be brought back by undoing all the day's changes (fixed: More has "Bring Back" for each). Deleting a child leaves her id in timetable blocks (not fixed).

How they were fixed, in short:

- 10: Practicing and Reviewing pick her row the way her cell does. 11 and 14: the block sheet saves only the fields changed, so a day's own change stays on that day and Save with nothing changed writes nothing. 12: a year-plan entry still waiting shows as Planned, and the Checklist's plan takes the entry rather than making a second plan.
- 13: notes about only the absent children go with them to their own plan, and Undo brings them back. 15: recall checks, work participants and year-plan entries refresh the grid (recall checks were missing from the sync notifications too). 16: the Children view counts work as the Checklist does.
- 17: a room whose name contains "adolescent" is the adolescent room; if no room's name does, any room counts, as before. Lanes have no field saying which room it is.

### Other features

18. **A child added while reviewing a capture takes an unplaced name's words and follow-up.** (`CommandBar/Services/CommandBarTypes.swift:210`)
19. **A weekday anywhere in a capture becomes the day the lesson was given, and "next Tuesday" is read as last Tuesday.** (`CaptureDateParser.swift:29`) Checked by hand.
20. **Siri's Log Observation can file a note on a child who has left** when her name matches exactly and a current child's sounds alike. (`Siri/LogObservationIntent.swift:45`; plausible)
21. **The note editor closes without a warning after the children, tags, "Include in report" or "Needs follow-up" were changed,** and throws those changes away. (`Notes/Editor/NoteEditorSaveLogic.swift:43`) Checked by hand.
22. **Changing a Book Club's day or start date wipes the leaders picked by hand.** (`BookClubSessionPlanner.swift:110`)

How they were fixed, in short:

- 18: each unclear name has its own Choose menu; adding a child with Edit no longer takes an unplaced name's words. 19: a weekday counts only when said as the date ("on Tuesday", "last Tuesday", "Tuesday" alone), and "next Tuesday" is ignored, since a capture records when a lesson was given.
- 20: a current child whose name sounds just like a departed one's wins. 21: the note editor warns about every field Save writes. 22: changing a Book Club's day or start keeps hand-picked leaders still on the roster; Regenerate still resets them.

Outside this pass's lines: a parent report's PDF includes flagged class-wide notes, which could name other children (`ReportGeneratorService.swift:46`).
