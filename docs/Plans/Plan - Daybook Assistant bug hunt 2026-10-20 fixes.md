## Apple guidance

Checked 2026-10-20 against Xcode 27.0 (27A266a), iOS 27.0 SDK, deployment target iOS 18.0. (Xcode 27.1 and the 27.2 RC change nothing here.)
- **A1. `UNUserNotificationCenter`** (#18, #23, #24): `removeDeliveredNotifications(withIdentifiers:)`, `deliveredNotifications()` and `notificationSettings()` are iOS 10+, not deprecated, safe from any thread; the async forms compile at iOS 18.0. Removal runs asynchronously in the background. Apple doesn't say they work while locked; nothing says they don't. [docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/unusernotificationcenter/removedeliverednotifications(withidentifiers:)) (2026-10-08).
- **A2. `NSPersistentCloudKitContainer.share(_:to:)`** (#7): imported from the Objective-C completion-handler form; no timeout, and **cancelling the Swift Task does not stop it** (SE-0297's continuation bridge). So the watchdog races the call and stops waiting; it can't cancel it. The abandoned call may still finish later: its result must be ignored by generation, and a later pass must tolerate records the late call did put in the share (the attacher already skips anything already shared). [docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coredata/nspersistentcloudkitcontainer/share(_:to:completion:)) (2026-10-08).
- **A3. `@SceneStorage`** (#22): iOS 14+, per scene, keyed by the string, so a value survives the root switching between onboarding and "Starting…" in the same scene. Types: Bool, Int, Double, String, URL, Data, Int/String raw enums. Cleared when the scene is destroyed (swiped away) — fine for a setup page. [docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/scenestorage) (2026-10-08).
- **A4. `TabView` + `scenePhase` + `.task`** (#1): Apple doesn't say whether a tab that isn't showing gets `scenePhase` changes, or whether `.task` restarts on reselection. So #1 must not depend on either: the day check runs on **both** the tab's `.task` and the scene-phase handler, and the "last shown day" lives in the view model (not in a modifier's `@State`). [ScenePhase](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/scenephase), [task](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/task(priority:_:)).
- **A5. `NWPathMonitor`** (#16): current; its `AsyncSequence` form (already used by `AssistantNetworkWatch`) compiles at iOS 18.
